# Delegated Engineering Governance Proposal

## Accepted Intent

The engineer accepted the preceding governance redesign and asked the primary agent to write its documents, assisted by independent Consultant, Reviewer, and Auditor contexts. The purpose is to turn the engineer's intent and key decisions, at the lowest practical cognitive cost, into a system worth having, correctly implemented, and maintainable. Automation, document completeness, and review counts serve that purpose. Preserve active human invitation when agents cannot determine a viable next path, resolve a consequential disagreement, or obtain required authorization. Fresh independent contexts address local convergence, repeated compaction, and implementation-context contamination. Self-correction remains useful; it is not independent acceptance.

## Current Facts And Proposed Direction

The current verifier mixes direction scrutiny with implementation failure, restart, and concurrency review. The auditor normally waits for a separately commissioned audit. Oracle rules treat fallible engineering judgment like a deterministic gate, while material-work triggers can turn weak scope signals into mandatory procedure. R058 provides evidence of useful earlier Consultant disagreement and probe-backed simplification; it does not establish universal operating effectiveness.

Use the existing governance owners and replace overlapping mechanisms rather than add a workflow controller:

1. Engineering Doctrine records the attention objective, intent fidelity, scheme value, implementation correctness, and the limited attention of every agent context. Human participation is a useful recovery mechanism, not a failure to hide or a budget to drive to zero.
2. Change Execution owns provisional consequence-based assurance, a proposal proportional to unresolved choices, independent Consultant scrutiny before substantial commitment to a new direction, probe-driven revision, and ordinary direct execution under an already-settled direction. Agreement records reasons and evidence rather than certifying optimality.
3. Replace the registered verifier with consultant. Consultant challenges value, premises, feasibility, simpler alternatives, and continued investment; Reviewer judges delivered correctness and completeness. They are not paired automatically. A context that co-designed a scheme cannot certify its independent final acceptance.
4. Preserve an independent recovery backstop for long-running work, but narrow both its trigger and its subject. After primary compaction, check before a direction-bearing commitment: a changed scope, accepted design, substantial new investment, irreversible/external effect, or closure whose delivered direction materially differs from the scrutinized scheme. An ordinary commit or cross-owner dispatch within that scheme is not sufficient by itself. Reframe, long resume, and primary replacement require a check before renewed material investment. The Consultant may name one concrete evidence/stage trigger for the next fresh observation in the checkpoint; reaching it pauses only dependent work regardless of the primary's self-assessment. No trigger is invented when no meaningful observable exists. Any role may raise a direction concern. The current harness still depends on actual dispatch; prose and tests cannot guarantee spontaneous intervention or fresh context. Do not add timers, numeric budgets, role quotas, or event schemas.
5. Incremental Auditor checks compare source intent, recorded decisions, current authority, and affected implementation/evidence, including relevant unchanged consumers. Within discretion left by existing authority the Auditor may settle a specific disputed assurance requirement, require relevant evidence, remove unsupported ceremony, or pause the affected commitment. It cannot waive a MUST, the Safety Kernel, an owner, or an engineer decision; invent intent; amend authority; or independently approve its own artifact. Routine findings use the current task context and an existing change record when present; only durable cross-task audit and measurement results require a dated audit record. Broad audit and task-external calibration remain complementary.
6. The primary may redirect or replace test-author, builder, and reviewer contexts using original intent, actual artifacts, evidence, and unresolved findings, without prescribing a passing verdict, hiding dissent, or overlapping writers. Stop the previous writer before replacement. Freshness does not itself prove independence or correctness.
7. Human invitation follows the cheapest useful authorized probe or independent direction intervention where available. Missing authorization is immediate; unresolved choice or inability to produce a credible next action remains a valid reason to ask. Pause only dependent work, provide a recommendation if one is supportable, and do not manufacture endless probes or wait for the user to notice stagnation.
8. Distinguish deterministic gate claims from reasoned engineering decisions; preserve real evidence and deliberate harness failure. Temporary probes may establish facts without becoming permanent observation infrastructure. In-work fidelity and negative checks use existing test/evidence paths. Remove the automatic per-material-change mutation requirement: formal calibration remains sparse, independently audit-owned and outside active execution, with isolated seeds and no mutation-score gate. A targeted intervention may still prove one deciding check under existing harness admission; it is not a calibration cycle.

## Scope And Ownership

Primary is sole writer of root AGENTS, Engineering Doctrine, Change Execution and its rationale, Documentation Model, Verification Instruments, relevant local guides, role registry and prompts, calibration/residue projections, focused governance tests, optional proposal directory admission, generated index, and this lifecycle's evidence. The three independent agents read and report only. Historical records and terminal specifications remain untouched. The unrelated R058 plan modification is another writer's work.

No product behavior, CI trigger posture, tool versions, documentation metadata schema, new coordination state, real-host action, commit, push, or publication is included. The current user instruction authorizes amending the existing governance owners; a separate product specification would create duplicate ownership.

## Deciding Observations

- Independent Consultant can identify a viable coherent route before the primary rewrites authority; no unresolved material scheme objection is hidden by agreement wording.
- Reviewer and Auditor inspect the actual complete diff against the user's accepted additions, identify no unresolved intent loss, duplicate authority, safety waiver, self-acceptance, compulsory role pipeline, or projection drift.
- Focused tests retain useful existing invariants and cover actual role registration plus bounded new instruction seams. Text checks establish projection consistency only, not live role performance.
- Documentation/lifecycle/index validation, focused Node tests, formatting, and whitespace checks run on the final scope. User-visible closeout distinguishes observed checks and independent judgments from unmeasured long-run effectiveness.

## Alternatives And Consultant Objections

The independent Consultant initially returned Reframe: narrowing the old verifier's question while retaining every commit and delegation as a trigger would preserve the excessive cadence. The adopted route narrows both purpose and trigger, retains a concrete prearranged independent observation, separates focused negative proof from formal calibration, and bounds Auditor rulings to existing discretion. After reading the revised proposal, the Consultant returned ACCEPT_DIRECTION for content SHA-256 `0dd180cdf428412d32246a91e4693c553dd54b228ca04654998599744f62560d`. That verdict concerns the scheme, not final artifact acceptance or measured operating effectiveness.

Retaining the verifier name saves a rename but preserves its implementation-verification framing. Replacing that one capability with Consultant is the smaller coherent responsibility model. A new coordinator, scoring gate, recurring intervention timer, or mandatory role sequence would create more execution machinery without evidence that it is needed. Broad audits alone detect divergence late; bounded in-work fidelity checks reuse the current task's evidence and context.

The engineer subsequently requested optional proposal retention in a change bundle so later agents can recover the reasons without reconstructing discussion. This record supplies that evidence. It adds no design authority, lifecycle state, version counter, or approval mechanism. Simple work may use a short plan, and a complete Draft specification avoids a duplicate proposal. The directory validator reuses the existing optional-evidence field contract and excludes the proposal from the generated index, with a regression for admission and omission.

Artifact review clarified the retention boundary: all material work follows Change Execution, while a durable record is needed only when context must survive or the engineer requests it. Incremental Auditor reporting therefore uses task context and cannot itself force a change record. This keeps the assurance intervention available for short consequential tasks without adding document ceremony.

## Remaining Assumptions

Fresh independent contexts can challenge a route but still depend on actual dispatch and available source evidence. Structural tests and independent review can establish a coherent instruction set, not sustained improvements in attention, efficiency, or drift prevention. Later task-external observation must evaluate those effects. The Reviewer and Auditor inspect the final artifact independently; this rationale does not accept itself.
