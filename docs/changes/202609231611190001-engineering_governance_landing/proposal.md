## Engineer Rulings

This section is the public account of the rulings the decision records cite, harvested from the temporary discussion record; quotations are translated from Chinese and summarized where marked.

- Pilot direction, 2026-09-22 (engineering quality pilot). The engineer asked that related functions be gathered one by one into the same deep module without affecting behavior, with DDD and BDD applied reasonably and redundant code removed through ablation. The engineer later clarified that the recurring Turn-status problem was already solved and was a sign of code starting to rot, mainly because agents in a growing codebase cannot always find code that already implements a capability. The engineer's direction was that the codebase itself, through its architecture, more than prompts or AGENTS.md, decides whether AI can work in it efficiently; that interfaces are left to the engineer's taste and implementation to AI and tests; that functional grouping should show in the filesystem; and that an agent should be treated like a newcomer who joins twenty times a day.
- R-001, 2026-09-23, summarized. Do not treat the residual credential exposure of the retention guard at capture or retention time; post-process it when a concrete use is known. Reason: the use of the data cannot be known now, so no treatment chosen now can be fully reasonable, and advance processing may be insufficient or may damage other uses. Revisit: before any export, training use, external disclosure, or cross-audience sharing.
- R-001 amended, 2026-09-24, summarized. The current purpose is internal use by the team; the most original and complete data is kept for later use; import and export do only what they strictly need; processing privacy and sensitive information before a particular use is outside this system's scope.
- R-002, 2026-09-23. The engineer broadly accepted the ownership and handling flow for system-level complexity signals, including triage by the Auditor, the essential and accidental split, and measuring the same signal again after a correction; the three structural findings from the change fan-out probe were recorded, not fixed, until the discussion closed. Reason: finish the multi-topic discussion before scheduling execution, to avoid scattered changes midway.
- Topic one, 2026-09-23, broadly agreed by the engineer. Independent review across a representation boundary relies mainly on executable counterexamples against conservation-style invariants, such as decode-then-check, reassembly across units, replay after publication, and resumption at a watermark; the evidence was four defects that a reviewer found with short boundary scripts while the builder's focused suites were green. A parallel dispatch names the single owner of each interpretation rule as well as each path writer. Excluded: a scoring framework as a gate, the pilot's central discovery hypothesis, fixed ten-round and two-round audit cadences, repository-wide Gherkin or mutation, and a 10–20% quota.
- Topic four, 2026-09-23, approved by the engineer. The pilot's observation framework does not become a fixed rule, because most of its items overlap existing rules. Two new points go into the reviewer and auditor contracts as questions, without the pilot's codes and outside the always-loaded root contract: what a caller must know or do to use a module correctly, and reporting raw before and after observations and their direction without scores or sums. Scoring and rubrics are excluded because boundary cases could not yet be judged mechanically.
- R-003, 2026-09-23. The engineer agreed with the whole analysis of topic seven. Specifications are classified by the role their decisions play into five kinds, concept, boundary, mechanism, topology, and process, recorded as a kind field rather than a new directory or type; the only use of the kind is a placement rule: concept-specific semantics stay in the concept's specification, and a cross-cutting mechanism specification owns the questions every member answers plus a member registry. A Draft may be organized by feature or problem, and the division happens at promotion to Accepted, where an independent verifier checks every criterion under DOC-015. The rebuild test becomes the root standard of documentation as the source of truth: after deleting all implementation, an agent could rebuild a system meeting the design and behavior expectations from the documents alone. It implies that normative text does not depend on implementation identifiers, that implementation-independent conformance tests at public boundaries are part of the executable specification, that specifications constrain boundaries and invariants rather than internal method unless an internal decision carries weight, that reasons are needed for a rebuild, and that a technology choice treated as a decision states its reason and replacement condition. The rebuild probe joins document mutation as a documentation governance instrument and is not run now. Reason: the standard is falsifiable and exposes specification gaps and coupling to implementation; like mutation testing it proves discriminating power by intervention rather than reading. Revisit: after the first real rebuild probe.
- R-004, 2026-09-23. One immutable file per decision under docs/decisions/, with fields for date, decider, decision, reason, rejected alternatives, revisit trigger, supersession, and affected owners, and the authority of an intent document. Scope: every engineer ruling, approved agent-initiated governing design change, and design decision whose rejected alternatives are likely to return. New decisions are recorded going forward; past ones are backfilled on demand, starting with a first batch chosen by a census. Inline ruling markers in specifications become links, so an unlinked Safety Kernel limitation is a finding. Reason: a single file is immutable and supersession is clear, a link beside the rule points to one file, cross-owner decisions are not duplicated, reasons stay out of the specification context loaded every time, and rulings are few.
- R-005, 2026-09-23. Temporary work packages carry a status header of at most ten lines; parked material goes to existing homes (an owner's open questions, a change record's findings and proposal, or only its status header when it has neither owner nor change); decision records hold only settled matters; harvested text is stripped of sensitive content; a read-only delegate proposes a triage and the engineer confirms each deletion. Backing temp up in another repository is out of scope.
- R-006, 2026-09-23. In-process composition of real producers and consumers is an important part of L2, not a variant; the primary names seams in the plan and the reviewer checks it; change execution and the test-author reading order change accordingly; no new rule is added. Reason: L2 asks whether both sides of a boundary agree; fixture conformance answers shape only indirectly, composition answers behavior directly, and the round-three defects sat in the former's blind spot.
- R-007, 2026-09-23. Accepted: harness-neutral role contracts under docs/roles/ with a README index, recorded in the recommendation as governance documents; capability tiers frontier, standard, and fast; each model name in one place with a platform reference for harness facts; default presets as starting points; no advisor role, with the Consultant declaring the gap it fills and its stance; and migration of references and contract tests. OpenCode is not supported; the first harnesses are Codex, Claude Code, Grok Build, Cursor CLI, PI through Herdr, and DeepSeek Harness.
- R-008, 2026-09-23. A situation index and tiered reading, with fast delegates reading only a bounded task contract; a Ubiquitous Language glossary for the engineering context, separate from product vocabulary, with classic terms and sources, marked project-specific terms, fixed phrases for overloaded words, and an avoid list, and a later optional terminology lint; leading words placed in role contracts; and a writer role that never changes a criterion, whose every rewrite another context checks. Reason: term collisions and overloaded words seriously harm readability and precision; naming classic concepts triggers behavior more reliably than describing it; text quality is a question separate from the reviewer's.
- R-009, 2026-09-24. The engineer accepted all of Claude Code's recommendations in topic ten: the two-axis role model, with positions dispatcher, primary, and delegate and functions researcher, test-author, builder, writer, reviewer, consultant, and auditor, the Orchestrator used only as a product term, copilot renamed delegate, no role added or renamed, and the primary free to perform any function while independent checks stay; Deep Module, separation of concerns, seam, and Conway's Law as the primary's leading words; ablation as an implementation self-check; the harvest of the pilot proposal; Gray-box Module as a glossary entry and a test strategy sentence; the selected DDD and BDD borrowings; fail fast and fast feedback loops; composition over inheritance, separation of concerns, and the open-closed principle as used in the recommendation; settled mechanisms stable and extension open, with durable records read by a tolerant reader that preserves unknown fields, unknown keys in hand-written configuration reported as warnings, authority-bearing and required features still failing closed, and a sentence beside NONNEG-001; over-design assigned to the Consultant at design time, the reviewer at each diff, and the Auditor across accumulated work; the documentation governance borrowings, the two-by-two of document ablation and mutation, and document complexity as an explanatory signal; the principles, preset, evaluation method, and trigger signals of concentrated maintenance rounds; the evaluation of the new framework; the leading-words allocation with its adjustments; and the revised form of the engineer's four documentation methods: design and governance documents under docs/ with local guides beside their directories, concept-based hub-and-spoke progressive disclosure with a soft length limit that triggers a split review, a tiered index merged with the situation index, and doc-first. The engineer also designated Claude Code as primary and writer for the landing, with Grok Build compilers and GPT-6 astra review.

## Choices Inside The Landing

- Role contracts became their own document type under docs/roles/, and the glossary, the writing guide, and the harness reference became platform references. Rulings R-007 and R-008 had accepted role contracts and the glossary as governance documents; the writer departed from that because the documentation model promotes governance into its own directory at a fourth member, which would move the three governance documents and every link to them. The writer returned this departure to the engineer as GOVLAND-FND-013, and the engineer accepted it on 2026-09-24. The platform reference set is now six enumerated files; GOVLAND-FND-012 records the choice between the enumeration and a directory.
- Decision records are not listed in docs/INDEX.md. The index locates owners, and a decision is reached from the owner that links it beside its rule; listing decisions would double the index without helping anyone find an owner.
- Thin adapters under .codex/agents/, .claude/agents/, and .grok/agents/ were first landed as ruling R-007 accepted, then removed on 2026-09-24 when the engineer objected to per-harness role files; a GPT-6 astra Consultant proposed prompt dispatch for every harness with launch-time model and restriction choices, which the engineer accepted, recorded in docs/decisions/20260924-prompt_dispatched_roles.md.
- The frozen governance corpus test was re-pointed from the Codex adapters to docs/roles/, and new text kept code spans to what names a file, command, or field, because the corpus ratchet counts distinct code spans and the landing had to stay under its baseline without raising it.
- Kind values for the 90 active specifications were first classified by a Grok Build compiler from each specification's Owns section and then applied by the writer; low-confidence cases go to the normalization plan.
- Inline engineer-ruling markers in specifications were replaced by links to decision records. Where a marker credited the engineer with a design the engineer had delegated, the new sentence says what was delegated.
- Intent Epoch was renamed Intent Revision in governance and new plans only; earlier plans keep their headings because recorded revisions are immutable.
- Two backfilled decisions have no owner statement yet. The interrupt ruling was stated in the worker control specification in this landing; the export ruling waits for an engineer answer on its scope (GOVLAND-FND-006).

## Consultant And Reviewer Objections

Six review rounds and one Consultant consultation, all through GPT-6 astra, are summarized in the plan's checkpoint. Objections that changed the landing: the role and glossary types departed from rulings R-007 and R-008 without approval, returned to and accepted by the engineer; the credential clause had strengthened a revisit trigger into a processing duty; several decision records presented inference as the engineer's reasoning or named sources that did not carry the decision, which led to the Engineer Rulings section; same-signal remeasurement had no operational owner; the interrupt ruling had swallowed the human-gate stop; complete export conflicted with the retained-body rule and then with the evidence owner's API restriction; and the first export regression had asserted unchanged references.
