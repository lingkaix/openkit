# Change Execution Governance

## Purpose And Scope

This document owns coordination for material repository work: preserving user intent, selecting and revising a method, composing agent capabilities, retaining enough evidence to resume, and deciding when to continue, reframe, ask the engineer, or close. It also owns the minimum content and lifecycle of material change records.

It does not own product design, architecture, test layers, evidence quality, documentation types, toolchain setup, or role-specific operation. Those remain with accepted Core and specifications, `docs/specs/20260529-test_strategy.md`, `docs/verification-instruments.md`, `docs/documentation-model.md`, `docs/toolchain.md`, and `.codex/agents/` respectively. A change record tracks work and never authorizes design.

Material coordination applies to cross-owner or cross-package work, public contracts, durable data or lifecycle, product workflow, architecture, deployment topology, governing authority, strict risk, multi-agent or long-running execution, likely future audit, or work the engineer explicitly places under a change plan. Ordinary scoped work executes directly under root `AGENTS.md`.

## Intent And Working Record

Start material work by recording the requested outcome, non-negotiables, acceptance observations, exclusions, effect boundary, accepted decisions, current facts, material unknowns, and links to owning documents. Do not predict a complete future queue, fixed role order, correction count, artifact inventory, or implementation magnitude.

For material long-running work, preserve the user's Intent as append-only Intent Epoch entries: a recorded Intent Epoch MUST NOT be modified, rewritten, or deleted. A change to the outcome, non-negotiables, acceptance, or effect boundary appends a new epoch with its source and reason. Current facts, unknowns, method, frontier, and Next Action live in a rewritable checkpoint. The checkpoint is a recovery aid, not authority or proof, and its consequential claims are reconciled with Git, the owned artifact, named evidence, or the relevant external system on resume.

A plan is a working prediction. Evidence may revise its method, decomposition, role composition, test order, or frontier without engineer approval when user intent, authority, strict safety, and accepted product decisions remain unchanged. Historical queues, matrices, assignments, gates, and state files remain evidence of what was once predicted or done; they do not control later dispatch.

## Adaptive Execution

The primary agent coordinates material work and keeps recorded Intent, accepted authority, and the Safety Kernel invariant while methods change. It reconciles reports with actual artifacts, chooses and integrates the next useful probe or action, assigns one writer per repository path, and delegates only when another context adds useful production capacity or independence. Its responsibility is convergence, not maximizing role calls or handoffs.

Registered roles are capabilities, not a fixed pipeline. Use a researcher for independent external primary-source evidence, a test author when accepted behavior permits an independent oracle to reduce uncertainty, and a builder when the owner, expected outcome, and writable boundary are clear enough to implement. Use review, verification, and audit as described below. No capability is a prerequisite for another, and none has a fixed order or invocation count; skip one when it adds neither useful capacity nor proportionate independence.

Review, verification, and audit remain distinct even though none forms a mandatory sequence. A reviewer is the normal independent acceptor for a material feature, bugfix, refactor, or documentation change and judges the actual artifact's correctness, completeness, owner alignment, and simplicity before acceptance. A verifier is used less often at a key blocker, a high-uncertainty claim, a major stage or plan close, or long-horizon recovery; it tries to falsify behavior, system coherence, design sufficiency, and the necessity of added mechanisms. An auditor is rarer and is commissioned by an engineer or an owning audit rule to examine longitudinal alignment among recorded user intent, accepted authorities, projections, tests, implementation, and runtime evidence, or to execute calibration and terminal-archive work. This document is the owning rule for an engineer-directed generic repository-integrity audit, whose audit record links this document; an audit generated by a more specific accepted owner links that owner instead. Audit findings inform engineers but never choose new intent, architecture, governing trade-offs, strict-risk acceptance, or final approval.

Before a material, delegated, expensive, repeated, or effectful Next Action, record what Artifact, Belief, or Decision it is expected to change, the expected Observable, and the Evidence that would change or reframe the route. Simple reads, searches, and obvious local checks need no separate record. After the action, record the observation rather than rewriting the prediction. Repeating the same method without a new hypothesis, evidence, or expected change is not progress.

Signals that the route may have stalled include inability to name the next Observable; report, handoff, or status churn without a changed Artifact, Belief, or Decision; conflict between a report and the artifact; recurrence of the same defect class across adjacent surfaces; or coordination machinery growing faster than the outcome. These signals are not counters, breakers, or automatic role calls. Respond with the cheapest direct probe or local correction, then `Reframe` when evidence defeats the premise or method, or use a fresh-context direction check when the primary context may be drifting. Resolve role disagreement against the accepted owner and direct artifact evidence, never by vote.

Every material turn has exactly four possible outcomes: `Continue` when an Artifact, Belief, or Decision changed and a useful next step remains; `Reframe` when evidence defeated the premise, plan, proxy, or method; `Ask Human` when a real intent, trade-off, authority, strict-effect, or residual-risk decision is required; or `Close` when acceptance is settled by direct artifact or observation. Activity, status updates, role handoffs, and gate narration are not additional outcomes.

Use `Continue` for bounded correction inside the accepted direction. Use `Reframe` before repeating a method that failed to produce its predicted observable. `Ask Human` states the decision the primary agent would take, why, and what evidence would change it; it does not present the same work in several procedural wrappers. `Close` reports exact evidence and unresolved findings without converting either into authority.

In material long-running work a fresh context MUST perform the direction check before a durable commitment whenever the primary context has been compacted since its own last check, after a Reframe, and on a long pause and resume or a change of primary-agent identity. A durable commitment is a commit, a `Close`, a cross-owner delegation, or an irreversible, externally effectful, or real-environment action. Coverage is per plan and per primary-context identity: a delegated context's own compaction is not the primary's, and a check clears the obligation of the primary context whose Intent, checkpoint, and evidence it read, not of the fresh context that performed it. Compaction frequency tracks tool-output volume rather than accumulated drift, so it is not a trigger on its own: where the plan keeps a route log, ordinary mid-slice compaction requires only one recorded line naming what was re-read and stating that no fresh check ran, and that line is self-reported rather than proof. The check reads the current Intent, checkpoint, actual Git or artifact state, and named evidence references, then returns only `Continue`, `Reframe`, `Ask Human`, or `Close` with its reason. This is a direction check, not a mandatory full review pipeline; the context that may be drifting does not certify its own recovery.

## Evidence And Acceptance

Facts come from direct artifacts and observations. Before accepting work, inspect the actual diff, bytes, or named execution output; a producer report is never sufficient by itself. A producer may self-review and correct its work but may not independently adjudicate an authority-bearing artifact it produced. Independent judgment is selected where consequence, uncertainty, or producer bias makes it material, not because a tier mandates a role sequence.

When an instrument or oracle will decide a gate, classify and admit it under `docs/verification-instruments.md`; never retain a gate by substituting a proxy that cannot observe its subject. A failing check proves the named defect only when the expected deciding path ran. Builders do not weaken, delete, skip, or bypass contract-derived checks to obtain green.

Verification scope is recomputed from the actual final diff. Run the lowest-sufficient focused checks while iterating; run broader package, repository, release, real-environment, or strict-effect checks when the changed surface or accepted boundary requires them. Preserve secrets, authorization, cleanup, external effects, and data-loss controls regardless of ordinary proportionality.

Findings outside the current outcome do not silently expand it. Record a finding with its evidence and likely owner; continue current work when safe. It becomes work only through user intent or an accepted owner. A defect within the current outcome is corrected directly or causes a reframe.

## Change Records And Retention

Create or update one change record under `docs/changes/` when material context must survive the chat or commit diff. A plan records its Intent Epochs, current checkpoint, owner links, accepted decisions, material evidence, current status, and eventual closeout. It does not need a future package queue or machine-readable coordination controller.

New change plans use a bundle directory named `YYYYMMDDHHMMSSssss-short_name/` with required `plan.md` and optional `findings.md` and `route-log.md`. A legacy `state.json` may remain unchanged as opaque historical evidence. Do not create, normalize, or append coordination events for the new process. No active authority may depend on a change record to interpret current behavior.

A `route-log.md` carries its plan's route history in one required shape and no other: one plain line per entry, appended by an agent at a decision point and never by machinery. The three approved pilot plans keep one; any other plan may, and none is required to. It carries four entry kinds and nothing else: a `Reframe` with the evidence that defeated the premise; a failed attempt or refuted hypothesis with the missing fact it named; a direction check, written by the fresh context that performed it, with its outcome and reason; and, for an ordinary mid-slice compaction, the one line naming what was re-read and stating that no fresh check ran. A commit already records the `Continue` outcome and the artifact, so neither is repeated here; a committing turn's defeated premise, refuted hypothesis, or direction verdict is still an entry. The primary agent owns the append, as it owns every other single-writer path. The file is non-authorizing execution evidence: `plan.md` keeps Intent, key design, accepted decisions, and the rewritable checkpoint, and gains no working log, while `findings.md` keeps this plan's findings and their dispositions. The entry kinds state what an entry carries, not a checked format: add no schema file, validator, test, revision counter, or automatic writer to it. Its completeness is self-reported and no check establishes it. At closeout the primary agent promotes its surviving conclusions to their owners.

A `findings.md` item has exactly three statuses and a new finding starts as `open`. `open` means the finding remains unresolved and the current plan still owes its correction, decision, or admission. `deferred` means the finding remains unresolved but an accepted owner or recorded engineer decision moved it outside the current plan and the last sentence of its `Next action` names the receiver and activation condition. `closed` means no work remains under that finding because an accepted correction, decision, supersession, or historical disposition settled it; `closed` is terminal, and recurrence creates a new item whose `Observation` cites the old findings path and ID. The permitted transitions are `open` → `closed`, `open` → `deferred`, `deferred` → `open` when accepted work readmits it, and `deferred` → `closed` when later work settles it. The item is the source when its index projection disagrees. A transition updates the heading and Follow-up Index in the same commit, appends its transition history to `Next action`, appends the closing fields when it reaches `closed`, and deletes no substantive history. The primary agent of the accepted change that performs a later transition takes exclusive write ownership of that earlier `findings.md` path for the transition.

Each finding heading is exactly `## [<status>] <ID> — <short title>`. For a new report, `<PLAN-PREFIX>` is one uppercase ASCII label matching `[A-Z][A-Z0-9]*`, selected once within that report, and every new ID is `<PLAN-PREFIX>-FND-<three digits>` unique within the file; an existing ID remains stable, including the historical `F-<number>` form, and no ID is reused, renumbered, or deleted. A citation outside the report names both its repository-relative `findings.md` path and the ID because IDs are not globally unique. Each item uses only fixed, non-empty, one-line Markdown bullets of the exact shape ``- **<Label>:** <text>`` and admits no other heading or body form. The fields appear in this order: `Observation`, `Impact`, `Evidence`, `Owner`, `Next action`, then `Closing verdict` and `Closure evidence` only when closed. Every item requires the first five fields. Open and deferred items forbid both closing fields; closed items require both `Closing verdict` and `Closure evidence`. `Observation` states the defect, `Impact` names affected owners and the consequence if it remains, `Evidence` names the observation and provenance without secret or untrusted raw content, `Owner` names the accepted receiver or says that none is accepted, and `Next action` is append-only transition history whose last sentence is current for an open or deferred item and whose complete text remains historical after closure. `Closing verdict` records the accepted reason no work remains, and `Closure evidence` names the exact artifact or observation that supports that verdict. A missing historical fact is written as `Not recorded.` rather than reconstructed.

After any permitted optional frontmatter, every findings report starts with `# Findings`, one non-authorizing preamble paragraph, and `## Follow-up Index` before its items, and admits no other headings except its item headings. The Follow-up Index is a non-authorizing projection and a marking surface, not the source of a finding: every `open` and `deferred` item appears exactly once in document order using the exact unchecked shape ``- [ ] `<ID>` [<status>] <short title>``; a previously listed item that later becomes `closed` remains in place using the exact checked shape ``- [x] `<ID>` [closed] <short title>``; other closed items are omitted; and an index with no unchecked or retained checked entries contains exactly `- None.`. The status and title are byte-identical to the item heading and the index uses no table. The current-snapshot check proves every unresolved item is listed and every retained checked line names a closed item; whether a checked historical line was improperly deleted is self-reported because a stateless corpus validator cannot reconstruct prior membership.

`scripts/validate-doc-model.mjs` enforces only this current-snapshot `findings.md` structure through the existing documentation-model check: document and heading grammar, status and ID vocabulary, ID uniqueness, exact field syntax, fixed field names and order, status-specific closing fields, and current Follow-up Index agreement. It does not decide whether a statement, owner, status, verdict, or evidence claim is true or sufficient, and it cannot prove historical index retention. It adds no schema file or automatic writer and does not validate `route-log.md`, whose unchecked one-line contract above remains separate.

Noisy transcripts, raw tool output, timing, temporary checkpoints, and intermediate evidence stay uncommitted under `temp/`. Retain enough raw material for the three approved pilot plans to support later task-external review, but do not install a new event schema, workflow engine, dashboard, or automatic blocker before repeated evidence demonstrates that need.

A plan's uncommitted material mirrors its committed bundle: `temp/changes/` holds one directory per plan under the same name as that plan's `docs/changes/` bundle, and that directory holds its scripts, probes, attempt evidence, and raw output. A plan may still reference and use other `temp/` paths where the work needs them.

At closeout, summarize what changed, link the accepted owners and commits, report exact verification, record unresolved findings, and set the lifecycle status accurately. Promote durable decisions and rationale to their actual owners. Change records are removable evidence and may be pruned after no active authority or plan depends on them.

## Commits And External Effects

Commit only with user authorization. Follow `CONTRIBUTING.md`; keep related changes coherent and use dependency order when independently versioned packages require it. Preserve unrelated user changes and do not amend, rewrite, push, publish, deploy, contact external systems, use credentials, or perform destructive cleanup unless the task and authorization cover that exact effect.

Real-environment work names the target, prerequisites, cleanup, retained evidence, and residual state before the effect. A failed attempt may still be useful evidence when it names the missing fact and proves cleanup and unrelated-state preservation, but it never becomes a product PASS by narration.

## Completion

Material work closes only when direct evidence settles the recorded acceptance observations, actual artifacts align with their owners, applicable safety and cleanup obligations are satisfied, and unresolved findings are honestly retained. The primary agent reports the diff, commands, exact results, external effects, and remaining uncertainty.

The engineer decides any change to Intent, architecture, governing trade-off, strict-risk acceptance, or important residual risk. Otherwise the primary agent continues, reframes, or closes without requesting permission for ordinary correction.
