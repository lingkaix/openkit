# Selected Skills As The First Internal Resource Family

## Decision

The first resource family delivered by #144 is selected Skills and progressive `skill.read`, integrated on command-initiated Assistant Turns admitted by `conversation.submit`. Workspace selection, reference validation, trusted assembly and operation invocation/wiring form one delivery unit. Separate pending-outcome Assistant continuation contracts remain intact, including their applicable continuation and failure requirements.

This bounded read-only family can satisfy #144 with its exact acceptance evidence. It does not complete the resource-integration specification or prove its wider MCP, effect-settlement, Kernel/UI and shared-loop contracts. Captured-effect approval and unknown external-effect non-replay are not applicable to retained Skill reads; they remain binding where another operation owns those concerns.

Standalone `chat.quick` gains no resources, request ID or synthetic Turn in this slice. Its direct Provider call remains an implementation divergence from the required shared-loop design. No permanent exception is accepted, and the shared-loop obligation does not wait for a future retirement decision. Retiring the entry or routing it through Timeline admission remains unresolved under the Chat owner.

## Reason

Source: the #144 scope entry dated 2026-10-15 in later-routing.md and the full independent Astra verdict in consultant-144-scope.md, both in the coordinator's backlog-20261015 working record. The Consultant agreed with conditions 2 to 4: preserve separate continuation admissions, deliver and verify one complete bounded resource family, report partial specification delivery, and retain the standalone shared-loop divergence. The coordinator commissioned this owner clarification under their agreement. The verdict accepts scope only, not implementation acceptance.

The existing conversation command supplies durable Assistant Turn and initiating Item admission. Standalone Quick Chat has no accepted command-to-Turn admission or replay contract. Adding that lifecycle is unnecessary for selected Skills and is not authorized by the Workspace capability changes. The four-owner implementation unit is P07 selection, P01 reference validation, P05 assembly and P12 invocation/wiring; module completion alone cannot establish the family acceptance predicates.

## Rejected Alternatives

- Treating conversation.submit as the only accepted Assistant admission would omit pending-outcome continuation contracts.
- Inventing a request ID or synthetic Turn for standalone Quick Chat would add an unaccepted admission lifecycle.
- Accepting a permanent direct-call exception would weaken the existing shared-loop requirement.
- Claiming full specification completion from one family or one module would exceed the bounded issue and its evidence.
- Holding the selected Skill family until the standalone entry is decided would unnecessarily block the already admitted command path.

## Revisit When

Return a new resource family or admission lifecycle to its owning contract before implementation. The Chat owner must resolve the standalone entry choice separately. Implementation acceptance still requires the bounded family's exact authority, immutable source, invocation, cancellation and Turn-evidence checks; this scope decision supplies none of that execution evidence.
