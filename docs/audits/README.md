# Audit Records

This directory contains dated evidence records produced by owning rules: calibration reports, drift findings, load-bearing maps, detection-rate trends, Core retirements, specification terminal transitions, and similar observations.

The audit-record type is owned by `docs/documentation-model.md`. A measurement record is generated by its owning specification, currently `docs/specs/20260719-verification_calibration.md` or `docs/specs/20260811-execution_residue_measurement.md`. A generic engineer-directed repository-integrity audit is generated by `docs/change-execution.md`; another audit is generated by its more specific accepted specification or governance owner. A terminal-archive record is generated by the archive rule in `docs/documentation-model.md` and the lifecycle guide for the archived document type.

A dated audit retains durable cross-task observations or a governed measurement. Incremental Auditor scrutiny, including a discretionary assurance ruling, uses the current task context and is retained in a change record when one exists or the context independently needs to survive under `docs/change-execution.md`; invoking the role alone requires neither record. Both are non-authorizing evidence. A permitted ruling derives from existing governance and cannot amend it or settle new engineer intent.

## Authority

Audit records carry no authority of any kind. They are observations that inform decisions recorded elsewhere: a finding that requires action becomes a change record or a specification update that links back to the audit record as evidence. Nothing may cite an audit record as design authority.

## File Shape

- Filename: `YYYYMMDD-short_name.md` with a lowercase snake-case name.
- Each record states what was observed, the observation date, the rule and owner that produced it, and the documents or surfaces observed.
- Each record links its generating specification or governance owner with a repository-relative path.
- A terminal-archive record names the exact source and final archive paths, the accepted transition decision, every authority criterion's receiver disposition, every inbound current-guidance link disposition, and each final archived-file SHA-256. It records the decision as evidence and does not authorize it.

## Lifecycle

Past records are never edited; a new observation is a new record. Superseded readings stay in place as the historical trend. Records may be pruned only under a retention rule stated by the generating owner; pruning is a normal commit, not a rewrite.

## Validation

`scripts/validate-doc-model.mjs` checks filename shape and the generating-owner link for every record in this directory as part of `check:repo`.
